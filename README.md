# Grove CRM

A production CRM platform for Iris & Oak Digital Studio, bringing internal client operations and an invite-only client portal into one system.

## Platform areas

- **Internal CRM** — manage clients, websites, care plans, projects, messages and operations.
- **Client portal** — give invited clients access to their own website information, care reports, check-ups and messages.
- **Site Check-ups** — create, review and send reports from the internal dashboard, with the finished report available to the client in their portal.

## Status

Planning and interface samples. The production CRM is not yet implemented.

The Website Care Report and Client Portal samples are separate visual examples. They are not connected to a backend and do not send or store messages, reports or client data.

## Planned domains

- `grovecrm.uk` — product landing page
- `app.grovecrm.uk` — internal CRM
- `portal.grovecrm.uk` — invite-only client portal

## Development

Development and preview deployments will use Vercel. The custom domains will be connected when the platform is ready.

## Privacy

Grove CRM is intended for Iris & Oak. Client access will be invite-only.
